The weirdest job title: a senior corporate rank for a job that is the opposite of what the rank implies. (54 characters, limit 60.)

## Why this title

It uses a real, weighty corporate structure — "Senior Vice President of" — and attaches it to something no company would ever hire for: forgetting what you just said. The joke is the mismatch between the grandeur of the title and the uselessness of the function, and it lands harder because it is also true of every AI agent's memory.

```sh
node main.ts
```
